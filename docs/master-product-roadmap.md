# Global Mobility Radar — master product roadmap v1

## Product contract
Personal-first, SaaS-ready international mobility platform for experienced professionals applying from their home country. Find direct permanent residence and temporary/long-term work routes, continuously monitor verified official rules and genuine employment opportunities, evaluate an editable profile, show evidence and next steps, and manage applications. Personal use comes first; no paid service or auto-application by default.

Retain React/TypeScript, Python stdlib collectors and SQLite where suitable. Start with a local-first independent runtime; add an owner-controlled API/database/host only when justified. No dependency on original author's private API, billing, authentication, deployment or legal identity. Preserve the upstream MIT licence.

## Release coverage
Primary countries: Canada (FSW, Express Entry CRS and draws, PNP), Australia (189/190/491, skills assessments, invitations/state nominations, employer-sponsored routes), Germany (Opportunity Card, EU Blue Card and settlement), Ireland (Critical Skills and general employment permits, residence pathway), Netherlands (HSM/Blue Card, recognised sponsor), New Zealand (Green List residence and AEWV).
Additional countries explicitly in original plan: UK (Skilled Worker/Global Talent and settlement), Hong Kong (QMAS/Top Talent), Singapore (Employment Pass and discretionary PR), UAE (job seeker/employment and renewable long-term residence), US (L-1/internal transfer, relevant sponsored employment and green-card caveats). Track visa vs residence vs PR distinctly. Every named route gets official-source-backed requirements and applicability screening or an explicitly documented non-deterministic/manual review outcome (e.g. discretionary PR); monitor official changes for each. No invented eligibility for discretionary schemes. Other inherited country entries may be displayed only as dated reference until validated, with no eligibility claim. Further countries are an extension, not an indefinite release gate.

## Sequencing (dependency-aware)
1. **Foundation gate:** document audit, isolate inherited workflows, safe CI, verify license/dependency baseline, decide reuse by evidence. No merge of unsafe deployment.
2. **Independent vertical slice:** local profile + generated job data + evidence-aware matching dashboard; eliminate private backend and paywall; test offline/empty/error cases.
3. **Trustworthy live jobs:** approved source registry, connectors, freshness/deduplication/verification and sponsorship, country-specific salary compatibility; add relevant verified employers.
4. **PR and immigration vertical slice:** Canada and Australia calculators first, then other primary and additional country screens; official rule provenance, test fixtures, dates and missing requirements.
5. **Monitoring:** official changes, rule approval/versioning, re-evaluation, schedules, source health, notifications and deadlines.
6. **Complete user workflow:** comparison, tracker, responsive dashboard and optional AI assistance; integrate and evaluate.
7. **Release:** security and privacy review, independent deployment, operational monitoring, recovery drill and complete acceptance reconciliation. Keep owner approval boundary for paid services/credentials and deployment.
