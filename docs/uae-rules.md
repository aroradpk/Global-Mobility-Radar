Jobseeker: 60/90/120-day visit, no sponsor, bachelor or equivalent and prescribed guarantee; federal either skilled levels 1–3 or approved top-500 graduate within two years. Dubai service page lists requirements differently, so explicit Dubai review; no assumed parity. Exact or older graduation boundary review rather than definitive pass. Official authority and evidence self-confirmed, no automatic university lookup. Visit is not work authorisation.
Ordinary mainland employment: employer verifies job offer, actual entity and permit process, pays applicable recruitment and entry/travel/residence costs. No blanket skilled salary threshold. Standard residence normally two years renewable. Government/free-zone/domestic and exceptional work permits manual. Medical, insurance, qualifications, identity and permit evidence independent.
Green skilled employee: five-year renewable self-sponsored residence, valid UAE contract, bachelor or equivalent, MoHRE level 1–3, AED 15000 monthly qualifying salary. Golden skilled professional: ten-year renewable, valid UAE employment and approved work permit, degree, level 1–2, AED 30000 monthly qualifying pay and comprehensive insurance. Dubai service additionally specifies six months bank salary transfers, degree recognition and required profession licence. Salary basis/FX and applicable issuing authority independently checked, no total package assumption. Other Green freelancer/investor and Golden investor/graduate categories are outside this skilled-employee increment; official guides linked. Exceptional digital technology talent separate manual nomination/authority review, no salary inference. None is unconditional PR or citizenship. No live employer, credential or authority validation; no automatic application or payment.
