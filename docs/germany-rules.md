Opportunity Card: full German/comparable recognition is the no-points route; partial recognition/compensation notice awards 4 and waives formal foreign-credential evidence requirement under §20b(1). Other points-route applicants confirm state-recognised credential (vocational at least two years), language A1 German or B2 English, 6-point minimum. German A2/B1/B2+ 1/2/3; C1 English 1; experience 2 in last 5 or 5 in last 7 (2/3 points, no stacking), shortage qualification 1, prior residence 1, jointly applying eligible spouse/registered partner 1. Completed age follows official Federal Employment Agency operational bands under 35 and under 40; check exact birthday boundaries with the authority because statute and portal wording differ. Funds benchmark €1091 per month for planned 1–12 months (€13092 for 12), with separately confirmed alternative evidence. Partial recognition is not full recognition. No automatic qualification, evidence, native-language, shortage occupation or prior-residence verification. Other eligibility conditions self-reported. Job search permission, not permanent residence.

Blue Card initial application: 2026 standard €50700 annual gross, reduced €45934.20 for qualifying shortage or recent graduates with required BA approval. Qualifying degree/comparable tertiary level-6 qualification with at least 3-year program, specific offer >=6 months, matches qualification and necessary licence. IT experience route needs at least 36 of last 84 qualifying months at academic level, ISCO groups 133/25, relevant to offered work and BA approval even when salary exceeds standard floor. Salary constants shared with jobScreening.ts and advertised salary comparison. No automated Anabin/ZAB lookup, NOC/ISCO mapping, pay component verification or actual BA decision.

Settlement: EU Blue Card eligible qualified employment/pension contributions 27 months with A1 or 21 with B1; other standard skilled routes 36 months and B1; German graduates 24 months and B1. Qualifying current residence and authorised job, livelihood, housing and civic requirements self-confirmed; no direct PR claim, no inference from years outside Germany. Special highly skilled direct settlement, EU long-term residence, self-employment and family routes remain manual reference/future work.
