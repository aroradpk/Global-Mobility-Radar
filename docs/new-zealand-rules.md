Seven screens: Tier 1 (age <=55, 30h, 12-month fixed or permanent offer, role-specific wage/qualifications, NZD35 default only if no specified rate); Tier 2 (24 months within 30, qualifying visas, historical pay and current pay confirmed; no automatic historic wage inference); ordinary AEWV (30h, accredited employer/job check, market pay, 24 months FTE or recognised credential, skill levels 3–5 English evidence, no job-offer payment). Contract-for-services cases, exemptions and historic wage transitions require manual review.
SMC rules effective 24 Aug 2026: single qualification/registration/income category, 3–6 skill points; NZ qualifying work 12/18/24 months => 1/2/3 points, cap at six, no double count. Overseas bachelor 4, overseas master's 5 with supporting bachelor, doctorate 6; NZ bachelor 5, qualifying masters 5 or 6, level 8 5 with supporting bachelor. Income current thresholds 52.50/70/105 NZD hourly; qualification/registration pay 35 (ANZSCO1–3) or 52.50 (4–5). Historical/grace basis manual. Work experience route ordinary non-Amber/Red ANZSCO1–3: 36 relevant + 24 additional NZ months, current 38.50 hourly; Trades/Technician: eligible role, relevant qualification and 30+18 months, current 35 hourly. Confirm lookback, actual occupation and evidence independently, no automatic occupational mapping/assessment or overlap computation. India Iconic/Skilled Work visa periods excluded from residence work experience. Current resident visa and permanent residence separate; PR physical presence 184 days in each preceding 12-month period, continuous resident visa >=24 months; alternative commitment routes manual.
